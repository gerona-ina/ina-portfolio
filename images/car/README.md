# car.kiv photos

Drop photos here with these names (any of .jpg / .png / .webp works, just keep the name before the dot):

| File | Used for |
|---|---|
| `cover.jpg` | Wide banner at the top of the car page (about 3:1, e.g. 1800×600) |
| `inari.jpg` | Round profile picture (square crop works best) |
| `suspension/cover.jpg` | Card on the car page + header image on the Suspension article |

For each new article, make a folder here named after it (e.g. `wheels-tires/`) and put a `cover.jpg` inside.
Keep photos under ~500 KB each (about 1600px wide).
