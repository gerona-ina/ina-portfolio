HV Enclosure + CAD Mockups

Enclosure layout and CAD for Caltech Racing's high-voltage system, with laser-cut mockups to check fit.

CategoryFSAE · Caltech Racing
StatusHanded off
RoleLayout · CAD design
ToolsCAD · laser cutting

Context

The high-voltage system needed a waterproof enclosure that fits its components and mounts within the chassis envelope. The enclosure would connect to the accumulator and the inverter, and house the main disconnect (essentially a kill switch), three large resistors, and other components.

Approach

  • Created the initial layout and CAD, modeling the electrical components.
  • Laser-cut plywood mockups to check the fit of the connector and component cutouts before committing to final material.
  • The final enclosure was planned in garolite. Nobody was trained to use the shop's CNC at that point, so I recommended prototyping and outsourcing the garolite parts.

Status

The mockups validated the layout. The project was handed to another team member before the final build.
